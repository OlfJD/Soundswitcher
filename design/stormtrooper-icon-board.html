SoundSwitcher: Stormtrooper look and app icon

Everything is drawn at the app's real size (the flyout is 320 px wide). The size bump and the blurry-text fix aren't on this board: they're bugs, and they'll go in whichever look you pick.

Reply with one letter for the look and one number for the icon, plus a colour from the switcher, for example C, icon 2 in coral. Tweaks are welcome ("D but with 1.5 px lines").

Stormtrooper look

Each option is white and grey with black outlines on every panel, tile and control. The colours come from Google's hardware (Pixel and Nest), not from the films.

App icon

These follow the same family as your other apps: a flat pastel tile with a dark navy symbol, no gradients or glow. The taskbar row puts each one next to MacroMaster and SmartNotes, and every size is shown, down to 16 px for the tray.
